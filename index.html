Featured case study: from a NASTP Alpha internship to a multi-standard platform

Sovereign QMS

The problem

Writing a management-system standard such as ISO 9001 or ISO 14001 takes consultants weeks, and companies can't paste confidential process data into public AI tools.

What I built

A full-stack platform: rules decide which clauses apply, a model drafts only the narrative, and every revision stays under document control. It supports ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 packs, and new standards are imported as data.

The outcome

Controlled document sets for ISO 9001 (30 documents) and ISO 14001 (18), with revision control, a Stage 1 readiness checklist, and a local-only mode so client data stays on the client's servers.

00s

Weeks of consultant work, compressed.

Here's how the platform gets from a company profile to an audit-ready document set.

0106

How it works

Six stages. Rules hold the facts, the model writes the narrative, and a person approves every revision.

01
Stage 1 of 6

Intake

An organisation profile and the applicable standards, isolated per organisation.

02
Stage 2 of 6

Scope

A scope draft is generated from the intake, revised section by section, then finalised with justified exclusions.

03
Stage 3 of 6

Requirement applicability

Rules decide which requirements of each standard apply, so applicability is never left to the model's guesswork.

04
Stage 4 of 6

Document set

The full document set for each standard. Numbers and clause references come from code; the narrative comes from a local or hosted model.

05
Stage 5 of 6

Document control

Draft, in review, approved, new revision, with an immutable snapshot of every submitted and approved revision.

06
Stage 6 of 6

Readiness

A certification-readiness checklist that updates from approved documents and shows what is still missing.
